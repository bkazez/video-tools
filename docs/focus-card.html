Assign the two buttons with the switch on MOVIE — custom keys are stored per shooting mode. Movie has no AF-S, so MF is the only state that cannot refocus itself; AF On still works from inside it.

Every take

  1. Frame first.
  2. Hold C1 on the stand-in. Let it settle. Release — focus is frozen.
  3. Press the center button, magnify, check.

Never zoom after focusing. The kit PZ lens is not parfocal, so the zoom drags focus with it.

Focus again after any power cycle. The zoom retracts and does not come back where it was.

A lens with an AF/MF switch stays on AF. The lens overrides the body and would kill AF On.
